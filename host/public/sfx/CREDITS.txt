  Two cries taken from the 22-second recording (at 8.70s and 9.75s) and joined
  into one call. Each is cut past its slow swell so it starts on the loud part,
  then high-passed to drop outdoor rumble, dynamically normalised so the whole
  call is loud rather than just its peak, faded at both ends and limited to
  -0.5 dBFS.
